Source: instagram.com/heavengatecamp (≈11.5K followers, 169 posts, reviewed 2 Oct 2026), plus public OTA listings (Booking.com mirrors).

## 2. Logo

A **gate/arch** outline in thin copper-bronze line, with a **four-point star** at its apex, **two mountain peaks** inside, and **water with broken reflection lines** at the base — the whole thing standing on a short horizon line. Set on a near-black navy disc.

Reading: *the gate between sea, mountain and sky*. The arch is the single most ownable shape the brand has — it reappears in their architecture (arched windows framing the sea) and in their post composition (photos shot through arches).

## 3. Photography & composition

| Observed | Implication for the website |
|---|---|
| Shots framed **through arched windows** ("Nuweiba is calling…", "The night everyone waits for") | Use arch-masked image frames as the signature image treatment |
| **Golden hour** sea with sun path on water; **Saudi mountains** across the Gulf | Warm amber/copper highlights, soft blue-grey sea |
| **Night**: Milky Way, Perseid meteor shower (13 Aug), moon path on black water ("this.") | Dark navy "night" sections with a subtle star field; one of two primary moods |
| Rows of **sand-coloured vaulted chalets**, warm wall lights at night | Sand / dune neutrals; rhythmic repeated forms (cards in rows) |
| **Palm-reed huts & thatched umbrellas**, wooden decks | Natural textures — reed, rope, wood — as fine line accents, never as kitsch |
| People shown **from behind, contemplating the view** ("Me + this view", "POV: you found your kind of escape") | Copy voice is first-person, quiet, POV; UI should step back and let the view lead |
| Minimal text overlays in **classic serif**, lowercase, ellipses | Elegant serif display type, sentence case, generous whitespace |
| Bilingual captions (Arabic + English), wellness retreat content, dog on the beach | Arabic is first-class; wellness + pet-friendly are real selling points |

## 4. Mood & personality

**Calm · Warm · Soulful · Unhurried · Quietly premium.**
Not a party camp and not a luxury resort: an *escape*. The emotional promise is "finally getting away from everything" — slow mornings, golden sunsets, clear skies, silence.

Voice examples drawn from their captions: *"Nuweiba hits different."* · *"Here, no plans."* · *"Some views just make you forget about everything else."*

## 5. Audience

1. Young Egyptian professionals & couples from Cairo (weekend / holiday escapes) — Arabic, mobile-first, Instagram-driven, pay in EGP (cards, wallets, Fawry).
2. Israeli travellers crossing at Taba — Hebrew, short-notice bookings, families & couples.
3. International/European backpackers and divers — English.
4. Wellness retreat organisers and groups.

## 6. Positioning

Upper-mid "boutique camp": above the classic EGP-budget Sinai hut camps, below 5-star resorts. Price should be presented confidently (from-price per night, all-in totals), never hidden.

## 7. What the website must avoid

- Generic "adventure camping" visuals (tents, forest greens, orange CTAs).
- Template hero with stock text and a centred button.
- Heavy UI chrome competing with the sea.
- Treating Arabic/Hebrew as an afterthought (mirrored icons, broken numerals, wrong fonts).
